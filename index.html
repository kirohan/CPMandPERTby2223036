Quick formula sheet

Use these formulas for CPM and PERT calculations.

Forward pass

EF = ES + duration

ES = maximum EF of predecessors

Backward pass

LS = LF - duration

LF = minimum LS of successors

Total float

TF = LS - ES = LF - EF

Critical activities have TF = 0.

Free float

FF = minimum ES of successors - EF

PERT expected time

te = (a + 4m + b) / 6

PERT uncertainty

σ = (b - a) / 6

variance = σ²

Completion probability

Z = (target duration - expected project duration) / project standard deviation
